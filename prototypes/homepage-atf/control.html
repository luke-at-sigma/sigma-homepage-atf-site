The AI Apps platform

Sigma is the runtime for AI Apps and agents on governed warehouse data. One control plane handles permissions, audit, lineage, and change management.

  • Warehouse-native by design: Sigma queries your data warehouse directly, so security and governance always hold at the source.
  • One workspace, every user: Anyone builds governed AI Apps in minutes with chat, a familiar spreadsheet, SQL, or Python.
  • Agents that take action: Deploy enterprise LLMs and AI agents that run real workflows across analytics, AI Apps, and your tools.
