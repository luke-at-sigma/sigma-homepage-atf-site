Spreadsheets and writeback FAQ

  • Can Sigma replace Excel for our team?

    For work that depends on shared, current data, yes. People keep the spreadsheet interface, and the data stays in the warehouse instead of in files.

  • Where does data entered in Sigma go?

    Input Tables write to your warehouse, next to the source data.

  • Is every change tracked?

    Yes. Input Tables keep a change history, so you can see who changed what and when.

  • Is there a row limit like a spreadsheet file?

    No file limit applies, because Sigma runs on your warehouse.

  • Who can write data?

    Only people you grant edit access to, under the same permissions and table policies as everything else in Sigma.
