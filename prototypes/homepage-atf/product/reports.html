Reporting FAQ

  • What is pixel perfect reporting?

    Pixel perfect reporting produces documents with exact layout, pagination, and formatting, built for print and PDF. Sigma builds them on live warehouse data.

  • Can Sigma replace our legacy report server?

    Yes. Teams keep the reports people still read, consolidate near-identical versions into one report with controls, convert existing workbooks into formatted reports, and move scheduled delivery to Sigma before retiring the old server.

  • Can one schedule send each person their own version?

    Yes. Email deliveries can run as each recipient, so every person gets a report filtered to the data they’re allowed to see.

  • What formats can reports go out in?

    Reports export as PDF or PowerPoint and can be scheduled to arrive by email or in Slack.

  • Can readers ask questions about a report?

    Yes. Reports are built on the same governed data as your workbooks, so readers can explore the numbers or ask Sigma Assistant a follow-up.

  • Do recipients see only their own data?

    Yes, when email deliveries run as each recipient. Reports follow the same row-level security as the warehouse and Sigma permissions.
