Questions people ask before rollout

  • How is this different from pasting data into an AI chat?

    Exports pasted into chat leave your warehouse and its controls. Sigma Assistant answers on live data under each person’s permissions, and the Sigma MCP server gives Claude and ChatGPT that same governed access.

  • Will people actually use it?

    People work in the spreadsheet interface they already know, or ask in plain language. Duolingo reached 91% active user adoption.

  • How does IT stay in control?

    Queries run under warehouse roles and row-level security, admins badge trusted workbooks and models, and audit logs record activity and AI conversations.

  • Can business users change data?

    Yes, through Input Tables. Entries are stored in your warehouse with a change history.

  • What license do people who only read dashboards need?

    A View license, which is built for report consumers. Your account team can walk through pricing.

  • Does Sigma copy our data?

    No. Sigma queries your warehouse directly.
