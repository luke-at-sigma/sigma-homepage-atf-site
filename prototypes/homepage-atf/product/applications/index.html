AI Apps FAQ

  • What is an AI App?

    An AI App is an application built in Sigma on live warehouse data. It can collect input through forms, route approvals, write back to the warehouse, and run Sigma Agents.

  • Who builds AI Apps?

    The people who know the process, usually analysts and operations leads, starting from an App Template, with Sigma Assistant helping build in beta.

  • Where does the app run?

    In Sigma, on your warehouse. There’s no separate server or database to stand up.

  • How does IT control what gets built?

    Apps use Sigma permissions and your warehouse policies, and admins control who can publish and who can see each app.

  • Can apps update other systems?

    Yes. Actions can write back to the warehouse or call other systems, such as updating records in a CRM.
